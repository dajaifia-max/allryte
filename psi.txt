Services Page
Heading:
Our Services
ADHD Psychological Testing
We conduct comprehensive ADHD evaluations for children, teens, and adults to ensure accurate diagnosis and tailored treatment planning.
ADHD Treatment
Our ADHD treatment programs include medication management, behavioral therapy, and coaching to improve focus, organization, and daily performance.
Anxiety Treatment
We provide cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), mindfulness-based therapy, and medication management to help reduce anxiety and improve coping skills.
Bipolar Disorder Management
Our psychiatrists and therapists work together to provide mood stabilization treatments and therapy for better emotional regulation and quality of life.
Depression Treatment
We offer supportive psychotherapy, medication, and holistic approaches to address the root causes of depression and promote recovery.
Medication Management
Our experienced providers assess, prescribe, and monitor medications to optimize treatment outcomes with safety and care.
Psychotherapy & Counselling
Individual therapy sessions to address emotional distress, trauma, relationship issues, and life transitions with a compassionate approach.
PTSD Treatment
Trauma-focused therapies such as EMDR and CBT to help process traumatic experiences and reduce PTSD symptoms effectively.
